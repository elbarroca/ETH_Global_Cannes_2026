## Lisbon Additions From Frontier

Add these filters to the existing Lisbon matrix:

- Founder-market fit: why this team should build this exact product.
- Ecosystem compounding: can another team reuse the package/indexer/template?
- Under-3-minute story: problem, product, demo, market, why now.
- Work-quality proof: especially for DePIN, service desks, and agent labor.
- Business wedge: first buyer, user, or developer adopter after the hackathon.

## Kill List

Reject for Lisbon:
- Generic consumer trading app.
- No open-source delta.
- Sponsor only as wallet/login.
- No proof artifact.
- No repo/demo.
- Market with no resolution edge.
- DePIN with no verifiable work/quality loop.
- AI agent with no budget, identity, or audit trail.
- RWA project with no credible compliance or distribution wedge.
- Multi-week startup concept that cannot compress into one 36-hour proof loop.
