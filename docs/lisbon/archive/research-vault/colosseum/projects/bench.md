## Purpose / Ideation

| field | value |
|---|---|
| one-line product | Bench is a platform for Opportunity Markets. Where Prediction Markets output public probabilities and resolve to a binary outcome, Bench aggregates insights, resolves subjectively, and rewards useful signals, revealed only to the market creator. Filling the privacy gap in information markets, Bench allows market creators to access high-value information without leaking it, creating a real competitive edge. |
| user pain | Market creators need useful signals without public leakage. |
| solution loop | Private opportunity markets aggregate, resolve, and reward useful signals. |
| why it likely fit Colosseum | Likely scored for market mechanism novelty and private information aggregation. |
| hackathon idea lesson | Lisbon market ideas need resolution and reward mechanics, not just betting UI. |
| reject if | Market with no resolution edge or user who pays for signal. |
