# AlphaDawg Option 1 Design Contract

## Foundation

- Canvas: `#070706`
- Surface: `#0D0D0B`
- Primary text: `#F5F1E6`
- Muted text: `#A8A49A`
- Brand: `#F4C542`
- UI font: Geist
- Identifiers: JetBrains Mono
- Controls: 10px radius
- Panels: 12px radius
- Motion: 120-180ms transitions, disabled by `prefers-reduced-motion`

## Brand and assets

Use the exact `DawgLogo` component and existing AlphaDawg raster assets. Use installed Phosphor icons for interface symbols. Do not approximate the logo with CSS, SVG, text glyphs, or emoji.

## Product hierarchy

Protected publication, protected hire, and authenticated job proof are primary. Drafts, examples, configured identifiers, legacy hunt data, iNFT, Hedera, Naryo, and screenshots are labeled as non-authoritative unless the matching per-job evidence exists.
